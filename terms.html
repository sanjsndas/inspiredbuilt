About Inspired Built Forms

An independent journal of interior design, architecture, and the poetics of built space. We publish long-form essays, practical guides, and critical perspectives on the art of living well.

Our Mission

Inspired Built Forms was founded in 2020 with a simple conviction: that writing about the built environment should be as considered as the spaces we inhabit. We believe architecture and interior design are not luxuries or afterthoughts—they are the backdrop of daily life, shaping how we feel, move, rest, and connect.

Too much design writing is either shallow trend coverage or impenetrable academic jargon. We aim for the middle ground: rigorous, accessible, and beautifully written. Every article is researched, edited, and crafted with care. We do not publish sponsored content disguised as editorial. We do not chase clicks. We take our time.

“The built environment is not a backdrop. It is a protagonist in the story of how we live.”

What We Cover

Our editorial scope spans interior design, architecture, materials, lighting, space planning, and the intersection of built space with culture, climate, and well-being. We profile emerging practitioners alongside established voices. We explore historical precedents and contemporary innovations. We ask not just what a space looks like, but why it feels the way it does.

Whether we are dissecting the science of circadian lighting or tracing the resurgence of lime plaster, our goal is to deepen your understanding of the spaces you inhabit—and to give you practical ideas you can apply, whether you are designing a home, commissioning a building, or simply noticing the world around you more closely.

Editorial Independence

Inspired Built Forms is entirely independent. We do not accept payment for editorial coverage, and we do not allow advertisers to influence our content. Our revenue comes from reader support, carefully selected display advertising, and occasional affiliate partnerships that are clearly disclosed. This model allows us to prioritise our readers and maintain editorial integrity.

We are committed to accuracy, transparency, and intellectual honesty. If we make a mistake, we correct it promptly and visibly. If we express an opinion, we label it as such. Our only loyalty is to our readers and to the standards of good design writing.

Our Editorial Principles

The standards that guide everything we publish.

I.

Depth Over Speed

We publish less frequently than many outlets. Every article is researched, drafted, edited, and fact-checked. We would rather publish one excellent piece than ten rushed ones.

II.

Clarity Over Jargon

Good design writing should be accessible without being simplistic. We avoid unnecessary jargon and explain complex ideas in plain language—without sacrificing nuance.

III.

Independence Over Influence

Our editorial content is never for sale. We disclose all partnerships and affiliate relationships. Advertisers have no input into what we cover or how we cover it.
