Before You Write

Quick answers to common questions about contacting us and contributing.

Do you accept guest articles? +
Yes, we welcome guest contributions that align with our editorial focus. Please send a short pitch (200–300 words) outlining your idea, along with a brief bio and writing samples, to editor@inspiredbuiltforms.com. We do not accept previously published pieces.
How can I advertise on Inspired Built Forms? +
We offer display advertising, newsletter sponsorships, and sponsored long-form content. Please email ads@inspiredbuiltforms.com for our media kit and rate card.
Can I republish an article from your site? +
You may quote up to 100 words with a clear link back to the original article. For full republication, please contact us for permission. All content is copyrighted.
Do you have a physical office I can visit? +
Our editorial team works remotely, but we maintain a mailing address in London (listed above). We are not open to drop-in visits, but we're happy to arrange a call.
How do I unsubscribe from the newsletter? +
Every newsletter includes a one-click unsubscribe link at the bottom. You can also email us at editor@inspiredbuiltforms.com and we'll remove you manually.
