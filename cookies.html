Cookies Policy

What cookies we use, why we use them, and how you can take control of your preferences.

Last updated: 1 March 2025

This Cookies Policy explains how Inspired Built Forms ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website inspiredbuiltforms.com (the "Site"). It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle your personal data more broadly.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to site owners about how the site is being used.

Cookies set by the website owner (in this case, Inspired Built Forms) are called "first-party cookies." Cookies set by parties other than the website owner are called "third-party cookies." Third-party cookies enable third-party features or functionality to be provided on or through the website (e.g., analytics).

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for a number of reasons, including:

  • Essential functionality: To make the Site work correctly, such as remembering your cookie consent preferences.
  • Analytics and performance: To understand how visitors interact with the Site — which pages are popular, how long people stay, and whether they encounter errors. This helps us improve the reading experience.
  • Preferences: To remember choices you make (such as display preferences) and provide a more personalised experience.
Our promise: We do not use cookies to serve targeted advertising, and we never sell data collected through cookies to third parties.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

The table below lists the specific cookies we use on the Site, their purpose, and their duration.

Cookie name Type Purpose Duration
ibf_consent Essential Stores your cookie consent choice so we don't ask again on every visit. 12 months
ibf_session Essential Maintains your session state across page requests. Session

4. Third-Party Cookies

In addition to our own cookies, we use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC. Google Analytics uses cookies to help us analyse how users use the Site. The information generated by the cookie about your use of the Site (including your IP address) will be transmitted to and stored by Google on servers in the United States.

Google will use this information for the purpose of evaluating your use of the Site, compiling reports on website activity for us, and providing other services relating to website activity and internet usage. Google may also transfer this information to third parties where required to do so by law, or where such third parties process the information on Google's behalf.

For more information about how Google handles your data, please see Google's Privacy Policy. You can also opt out of Google Analytics across all websites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. How to Control Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can set or amend your web browser controls to accept or refuse cookies. If you choose to reject cookies, you may still use our Site, though your access to some functionality and areas may be restricted.

Here's how to manage cookies in the most common browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data.

To find information relating to other browsers, visit the browser developer's website. You can also visit www.allaboutcookies.org for comprehensive guidance.

Quick tip: Most browsers let you browse in "private" or "incognito" mode, which limits the cookies stored on your device. However, this may affect the functionality of some websites.

6. Changes to This Cookies Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.

Cookie Questions

Quick answers to common questions about cookies and tracking on our site.

Do cookies track my personal identity? +
No. The cookies we use collect anonymous, aggregated data. They don't identify you personally — we only see trends like which pages are popular or how long visitors stay.
Can I use the site without cookies? +
Yes, you can browse the Site with cookies disabled. However, some features — such as remembering your preferences — may not work as intended.
How do I delete cookies already stored? +
You can clear cookies through your browser settings. Look for "Clear browsing data" or "Cookies and site data" in your browser's privacy or history menu.
Do you use cookies for advertising? +
No. We do not use advertising cookies or sell any data collected through cookies. Our cookies are strictly for functionality, analytics, and preferences.
How often is this policy updated? +
We review our cookies policy at least annually and whenever we make material changes to the cookies we use. The "Last updated" date at the top always reflects the latest version.
